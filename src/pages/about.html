Why we built it

Most material calculators stop at square footage. Real projects have L-shaped rooms, doors and windows, waste from cuts, and products sold in boxes, bags, tins and gallons. Buying too little means a second trip mid-job; buying too much wastes money on materials you may not be able to return.

So every calculator here handles odd shapes, adds the extra the trades plan for, rounds to the units the store actually sells, and gives you a shopping list you can print or copy to your phone. Measurements work in feet and inches or metres and centimetres, and prices and pack sizes follow the country you choose, so a visitor in India sees litre tins and 50 kg cement bags rather than gallons and 80 lb bags.

How our guides are written

Our project guides are written under the byline Noah Scott, the name we use for the {{site.name}} editorial desk. Each guide follows the same steps:

  1. Start from a real question. We look at what homeowners are searching for right now, such as a new color of the year, a seasonal job or a cost that has changed, and pick topics where a clear answer with numbers is missing.
  2. Research from primary sources. Coverage rates, bag yields and pack sizes come from manufacturer data sheets and labels. Seasonal and lawn advice comes from university extension services. Structural limits come from building standards such as the International Residential Code. Sources are listed in each guide.
  3. Work the numbers. Every quantity in a guide is worked out with the matching calculator and checked by hand, so the guide and the calculator always agree.
  4. Review before publishing, and update the guide when products, prices or advice change. The date at the top of each guide shows when it was last updated.

How we keep the numbers right

  • Every formula is published on our How we calculate page, with the assumptions behind it.
  • Coverage figures, such as bag yields and paint coverage, come from typical manufacturer labels, and you can change them to match the product you buy.
  • Each calculator is checked against hand-worked examples in our test suite.
  • If you spot a mistake, tell us through the contact page. We check every report and correct errors promptly.
