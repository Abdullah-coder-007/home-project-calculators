Good reasons to write

  • A result looks wrong. Use the Share this result button on the calculator and paste the link into your email, so we can see exactly what you entered. Tell us what you expected and where that number came from, such as the label on the product.
  • A guide has a mistake or is out of date. Send the page link and the part that needs fixing. We check every report against the source and update the page.
  • You want a new calculator. Tell us the project and what you need to buy for it. Many of our calculators started as reader requests.
  • You want to embed a calculator on your blog or business site. Embedding is free; the code is at the bottom of each calculator page.
  • Privacy questions about the data described in our privacy policy.

What to include

The page link, the device and browser you used, and a screenshot if something displays oddly. For prices or pack sizes, tell us your country and the store, since these vary from place to place.

What we can't help with

We cannot give project-specific engineering or code advice, inspect work, or recommend a particular contractor. For structural questions, please contact a licensed contractor or your local building department.

Advertising and partnerships

We don't accept paid reviews or sponsored results. For other partnership questions, use the same email address and put "Partnership" in the subject line.
