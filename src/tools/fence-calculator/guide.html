How the fence calculator works

Posts = fence length ÷ post spacing, rounded up, + 1
Rails = (posts − 1) × rails per section
Pickets = fence length (in) ÷ (picket width + gap), rounded up
Concrete per post = hole volume − the part of the 4x4 post inside it

Worked example

A 100 foot fence with posts every 8 feet needs 14 posts and 13 sections, so 26 rails with 2 rails each. With 5.5 inch pickets and 1/2 inch gaps, 200 pickets. Ten-inch holes 30 inches deep hold about 1.15 cubic feet of concrete each around the post, so 14 posts need 30 bags of 80 lb mix with 10% extra.

Post length

Posts must be the fence height plus the depth in the ground. A 6 foot fence in 30 inch holes needs 8.5 foot posts, so the calculator lists 10 foot posts. Cut the tops level after setting them.

Before you dig

  • Call 811 a few days before digging so utility lines are marked for free.
  • Confirm your property line and any HOA or city height limits.
  • Use ground-contact rated posts and exterior screws or galvanized nails.

Sources

Hole size and the 811 reminder above come from these pages. Frost depth, soil and local rules change the right hole depth, so adjust for your site.
