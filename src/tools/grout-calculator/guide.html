How the grout calculator works

Grout fills the joints between tiles, all the way down to the thinset. So the amount depends on how much joint there is per square foot: small tiles, wide joints and thick tiles all need more. The calculator treats one tile plus its joint as a repeating block, works out the joint volume, and turns it into pounds or gallons.

Block = (tile length + joint) × (tile width + joint)
Joint volume per sq ft (cu in) = 144 × (block − tile length × tile width) × tile thickness ÷ block
Grout = area × joint volume × (1 + extra %)
Sanded: 0.065 lb per cu in. Non-sanded: 0.062 lb per cu in. Pre-mixed: about 220 cu in per gallon

The weights per cubic inch were matched to Custom Building Products' published coverage charts. For common tile sizes the calculator comes within about 2% of those charts; for small mosaics it errs slightly on the generous side.

Worked example

A 100 sq ft bathroom floor uses 12 by 12 in tile, 3/8 in thick, with 1/8 in joints. Each block is 12 1/8 by 12 1/8 in, so the joints hold 1.11 cubic inches per sq ft. That is 111 cubic inches, plus 10% is 122 cubic inches, or 7.9 lb of sanded grout. That is just over a 7 lb bag, so buy one 25 lb bag. In non-sanded grout with 1/16 in joints it is 3.8 lb, one 10 lb box; in pre-mixed grout with 1/8 in joints it is 0.55 gallons, one gallon pail.

Choosing a grout

Sanded1/8 to 1/2 in25 lb and 7 lb bagsStrong and cheap; sand can scratch polished stone and glass

Joint ranges are from Custom Building Products' Polyblend Plus and SimpleGrout data. Other brands come in other sizes, such as 8 lb and 25 lb bags; their charts give similar coverage per pound.

Before you buy

  • Measure a joint after setting the tile; it is the spacer size only if you used spacers throughout.
  • Clean thinset out of the joints before it hardens so the grout can fill the full depth.
  • Buy every bag in one color batch, and mix only what you can use in about 30 minutes.
  • Seal cement grout after it cures if the maker recommends it, especially in showers and kitchens.
