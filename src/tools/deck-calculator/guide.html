How the deck calculator works

Rows of boards = deck width (in) ÷ (board width + gap), rounded up
Linear feet = rows × deck length
Boards = linear feet × (1 + waste) ÷ board length, rounded up
Joists = deck length (in) ÷ spacing + 1
Screws = rows × joists × 2

Worked example

A 16 by 12 foot deck with 5.5 inch boards and 1/8 inch gaps needs 26 rows (144 ÷ 5.625). That is 416 linear feet, or 29 boards of 16 feet with 10% extra. Joists 16 inches apart along 16 feet: 13 joists, 12 feet long. Screws: 26 × 13 × 2 = 676.

What this does not cover

Beams, posts, footings, the ledger board, railings and stairs carry the deck's load and are set by your local building code and span tables. Most areas require a permit for a deck. Use the stairs calculator for steps and the concrete calculator for footings.

Tips

  • Choose board length so joints land on joists and are staggered from row to row.
  • Use screws rated for treated lumber or the composite brand you use.
