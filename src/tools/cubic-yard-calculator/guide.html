How to calculate cubic yards

A cubic yard is a box 3 feet on every side, or 27 cubic feet. Bulk soil, gravel, mulch and concrete are ordered this way.

Cubic feet = area (sq ft) × depth (ft)
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Cubic meters = cubic feet × 0.0283

Worked example

A 10 by 10 foot area filled 6 inches deep: 100 sq ft × 0.5 ft = 50 cubic feet. 50 ÷ 27 = 1.85 cubic yards, so order 2 yards.

Tips for ordering

  • Loose materials such as soil and gravel settle when compacted. Add 5 to 10% if you will tamp or roll them.
  • Most suppliers deliver in 1/2 yard or 1 yard steps and charge a delivery fee for small loads.
  • For bags, weights and waste for a specific material, use the concrete, mulch, soil and gravel or paver calculators.
