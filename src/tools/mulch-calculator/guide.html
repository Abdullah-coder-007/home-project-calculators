How the mulch calculator works

Landscape materials are sold by volume (bags in cubic feet, bulk in cubic yards) or, for stone and sand, by weight. The calculator finds the volume of your bed or path and converts it into each way you can buy.

Cubic feet = area (sq ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Bags = cubic feet ÷ bag size, rounded up
Tons = cubic yards × weight per cubic yard

Worked example

A 20 by 10 foot flower bed is 200 sq ft. At 3 inches deep that is 50 cubic feet, or 1.85 cubic yards. In 2 cu ft bags that is 25 bags; in bulk, order 2 yards.

Measuring beds and paths

  1. Rectangular beds: measure length and width.
  2. Round tree rings: choose Circle and enter the diameter. Subtract the trunk with a small circle cut-out if it is large.
  3. Curved or kidney-shaped beds: break them into a rectangle plus half-circles or triangles, or measure the longest length and average width.
  4. Paths: total length by width. Add each branch as its own section.

Before you buy

  • Bulk deliveries usually have a minimum order and a delivery fee; ask before comparing with bag prices.
  • Have the delivery dumped on a tarp near where you need it to save wheelbarrow trips.
  • Check your vehicle's payload before hauling gravel or sand yourself. One cubic yard of gravel is well over a ton.
