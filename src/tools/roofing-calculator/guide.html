How the roofing calculator works

A sloped roof has more area than the ground under it. The calculator multiplies the footprint, including the overhang, by a pitch factor.

Footprint = (length + 2 × overhang) × (width + 2 × overhang)
Pitch factor = √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²)
Roof area = footprint × pitch factor
Bundles = roof area ÷ 100 × (1 + waste) × 3, rounded up

Worked example

A 40 by 30 foot house with a 12 inch overhang has a 42 by 32 foot footprint: 1,344 sq ft. A 6/12 pitch has a factor of 1.118, so the roof is 1,503 sq ft, or 15 squares. With 10% waste for a gable roof that is 16.5 squares, or 50 bundles.

Materials list

  • Shingles: 3 bundles per square for most three-tab and architectural shingles.
  • Nails: about 320 per square (4 per shingle); 480 in high-wind areas.
  • Underlayment: covers the whole roof deck. Rolls vary from about 400 to 1,000 sq ft.
  • Drip edge: runs around the roof perimeter in 10 foot pieces.
  • Starter strip and ridge cap: bought by length; check the coverage on the bundle.

Safety

Roof work causes serious falls every year. Use proper fall protection, never work on a wet or icy roof, and consider hiring a licensed roofer for steep pitches.

Sources

The 100 square foot roofing square and the three bundles per square are the standard figures for most asphalt shingles. Some specialty shingles use more, so check the bundle label before you order.
