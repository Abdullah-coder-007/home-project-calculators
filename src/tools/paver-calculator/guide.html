How the paver calculator works

Pavers = area × (1 + waste) ÷ area of one paver
Base = (area + edge length × base depth) × base depth, in cubic yards; tons = cubic yards × 1.4
Sand = area × sand depth ÷ 0.5 cu ft per bag

Worked example

A 12 by 12 foot patio is 144 sq ft. Each 4 by 8 inch paver covers 0.222 sq ft, so with 10% extra you need 713 pavers. A 4 inch base that runs past the 48 feet of edges is about 1.98 cubic yards, or 2.8 tons of crushed stone. One inch of sand is 12 cubic feet, or 24 bags.

Before you buy

  • Order pavers from one production run so colors match, and keep a few spares.
  • Rent a plate compactor; hand tamping rarely gets a base firm enough.
  • Slope the patio about 1/4 inch per foot away from the house for drainage.
