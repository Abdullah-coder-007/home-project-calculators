How to work out square footage

Square footage is the area of a flat surface measured in square feet. Each section is worked out on its own and added up, and cut-out sections are subtracted.

Rectangle = length × width
Triangle = ½ × base × height
Circle = 3.1416 × (diameter ÷ 2)²

Worked example

A 15 by 12 foot living room is 15 × 12 = 180 sq ft. That is 20 square yards (180 ÷ 9) or 16.72 square meters. At $3 per square foot, materials cost $540.

Measuring tips

  1. Measure wall to wall at floor level, in feet and inches.
  2. Round each measurement up to the next inch rather than down.
  3. Split L-shaped and T-shaped rooms into rectangles that do not overlap.
  4. For a bay window, add the bay as its own rectangle or triangle.
  5. Subtract fixed items you will not cover, such as a kitchen island, with a cut-out section.
