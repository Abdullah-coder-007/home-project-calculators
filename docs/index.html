Know how much to buy before you drive to the store.

Free calculators for paint, concrete, tile, flooring and mulch. Enter your measurements, get a shopping list with quantities and a cost estimate, and print it for the store.

  • Paint calculatorGallons of wall and ceiling paint, primer and tape for a room
  • Concrete calculatorBags or cubic yards for slabs, footings and post holes
  • Tile calculatorTiles, boxes, thinset and grout for floors, walls and backsplashes
  • Flooring calculatorBoxes of laminate, vinyl plank or hardwood, plus underlayment and trim
  • Mulch, soil and gravel calculatorBags, cubic yards or tons of mulch, topsoil, gravel and sand

Odd-shaped rooms work

Add L-shapes, triangles and circles, and subtract islands or built-ins. Most calculators only handle simple rectangles.

Waste is built in

Every result includes extra for cuts, spills and breakage, using the allowances pros use. You can change it.
