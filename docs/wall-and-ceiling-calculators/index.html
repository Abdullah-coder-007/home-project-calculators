Wall and ceiling calculators

Planning to paint, hang drywall or wallpaper a room? Each calculator takes your room size in feet and inches, subtracts doors and windows, adds the waste the trades plan for and tells you what to buy in store units: gallons and quarts, 4 by 8 or 4 by 12 sheets, or double rolls. Insulation is worked out in bags of batts or blown-in fill for the R-value you want, and baseboard and casing in 8, 12 or 16 ft pieces. The brick calculator counts modular, queen, king, Norman or utility brick for a wall, with bags of mortar mix and veneer ties.
