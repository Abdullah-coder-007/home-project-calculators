Floor calculators

Measure once and get the square footage, the number of boxes or tiles and the extras you need for a floor. The calculators handle L-shaped rooms and cut-outs, add extra waste for diagonal and herringbone tile layouts, and round up to full boxes after the waste is added. The grout calculator turns your tile size, joint width and tile thickness into pounds and bags of sanded or non-sanded grout, or pails of pre-mixed.
